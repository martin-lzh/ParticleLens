# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in ParticleLens a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, constructive feedback, respect for
different viewpoints, accountability, and a focus on what is best for the
community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, sexualized
attention, insults, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information,
or other conduct that would reasonably be inappropriate in a professional
setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the
contact method in the repository's security policy. Reports will be reviewed
promptly and fairly, with respect for the reporter's privacy.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this code.
